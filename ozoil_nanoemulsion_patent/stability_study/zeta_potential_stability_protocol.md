## 4. Zeta potential method

- Instrument: Malvern Zetasizer Nano-ZS (or equivalent) with folded capillary cell DTS1070; 25 °C; Smoluchowski model.
- Dilution: 1:100 in 1 mM NaCl adjusted to the sample pH (record pH and conductivity of the diluent). Do not dilute in deionised water alone, since conductivity below about 0.05 mS/cm gives unstable readings.
- Equilibration 120 s; 3 runs per vial, each ≥ 12 sub-runs; report mean ± SD of the 3 vials.
- System suitability: zeta transfer standard DTS1235 (−42 ± 4.2 mV) at the start of each session; record result.
- Acceptance criterion proposed for the specification: −25 mV to −50 mV at every time point; change from T0 not more than 10 mV in absolute value.

## 6. How the results enter the application

- Measured values replace the prophetic Example 7 in the draft, which is then re-written in the past tense.
- Keep raw Zetasizer export files (.dts / .csv) and the instrument audit trail; priority-date evidence may be requested during examination or litigation.
- If any batch falls outside −25 to −50 mV or drifts more than 10 mV, report it: a selective presentation of data is as damaging as a fabricated one.
